Source Methodology

Methodology

The calculator divides the total cost of a government project by the average UK family's lifetime tax contribution:

Project Cost ÷ £1,277,580 = Families

The figure of £1,277,580 is the average UK family's total tax contribution over a lifetime, sourced from the TaxPayers' Alliance.

The result is rounded to the nearest whole number. It represents how many families would need to dedicate their entire lifetime of tax payments to fund a single project.

Project costs are sourced individually — click "Source" on each project card for details.
